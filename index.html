Yard Management User Guide

How to load a truck using the Yard Management iPad app version 16.1 as of May 16, 2026 — A step-by-step walkthrough · SCM

STEP 1

Clock In

Confirm you're clocked in, then open Yard Management.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Your login
    Your name and email show at the top so you know you're signed in to the right account.
  2. 2
    Clock-in status
    “Out of Terminal” shows your current state and your home terminal mileage. It updates once you start a job.
  3. 3
    Clock Out button (green = clocked in)
    The button is green and labeled Clock Out because you're already clocked in. Only press it at the end of your shift.
  4. 4
    Lunch breaks
    Tap Lunch 1 Start or Lunch 2 Start to log a meal break. Payroll Time keeps running across the bottom.
  5. 5
    Tap Yard Management
    From here you'll choose Yard Management to start loading a truck. Fuel Loads and Container Onsite Check aren't covered in this guide.
STEP 2

Select Terminal & Open Loads

Tap your terminal, then choose Planned Loads or All Loads.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Back arrow
    Returns you to the Clock In screen if you opened Yard Management by mistake.
  2. 2
    Tap your terminal
    Pick the location you're working today — Napa here. The row turns orange to confirm your choice.
  3. 3
    Plan Date
    Defaults to today. If you're pre-loading tomorrow's trucks, tap the pill and set it to tomorrow's date — Planned Loads then shows what's scheduled for that day.
  4. 4
    Planned Loads
    Loads the transportation department has scheduled onto house trucks for the selected date.
  5. 5
    Tap All Loads
    Everything in the queue — house trucks plus will-call trucks. We use All Loads in this example.
STEP 3

Choose a Load

Find the load you're going to pull and tap to open it.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Search
    Type a load, BOL, or shipper number to filter the list when there are dozens of loads to scan.
  2. 2
    Terminal banner
    Confirms which yard's loads you're looking at — Napa (Fontana) here.
  3. 3
    Load number
    The bold value next to “Load:” is the primary identifier. Customer numbers (1039697), shipper formats (1039965BOX), and system-generated codes (P1278433) all appear together.
  4. 4
    Assigned Driver
    Tells you who's picking up. A name (e.g., Villa, Pancho) is a scheduled house-truck driver. “will call” means a will-call truck — the customer's own driver. No line at all means no driver yet.
  5. 5
    Tap a load to open
    Tap anywhere on the row to open the load details. In this example we'll tap 860482BOX (highlighted).
STEP 4

Load Details

Open the menu (top right) to access load actions.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Tap the menu (top right)
    Opens the load actions menu — that's the next screen and where you'll go from here.
  2. 2
    Load details
    Mostly reference numbers — Load, BOL, Shipper Number, Payor Ref, etc. Use these to confirm you opened the right load.
  3. 3
    Driver Signature
    Greyed out — you can't collect the driver's signature until the load is marked complete.
  4. 4
    Select BOL Items
    Opens the list of items on the bill of lading so you can mark each one as loaded.
  5. 5
    Loading Complete
    Marks the load as fully loaded. Only tap this once every item is on the truck.
STEP 5

The Action Menu

Tap Assign to Driver — the first step in loading a truck.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    The Action Menu
    Tapping the hamburger opens this list of load actions. We'll cover each one as we use it — two are skipped in this guide, the rest are coming up step by step.
  2. 2
    Skipped today
    Assign to Warehouse Staff is for a manager assigning the load to specific staff. Set Stage Location flags where the staged items wait for the truck. Both are typically handled before you arrive at the load.
  3. 3
    Tap Assign to Driver
    The first step in loading a truck — the driver must be on the load before anything else happens. Some loads already show a name from the office (see Step 3); if yours doesn't, you assign it here.
STEP 6

Select the Driver

Pick the driver, then tap Assign.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Select Driver screen
    Tapping Assign to Driver brings you here — the list of drivers available for your terminal.
  2. 2
    Back
    Returns you to the action menu without making a change.
  3. 3
    Search
    Type a name or phone number to filter the list when you have a lot of drivers to scan through.
  4. 4
    Choose a driver
    Tap the driver hauling this load — Chuck Norris in this example. The row turns orange to confirm your selection.
  5. 5
    Tap Assign
    Locks the driver onto the load. The app returns to Load Details with the driver now populated.
STEP 7

Start Loading the Truck

Three taps, in this order: Mark as LogIn → Start Loading → Select BOL Items.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Mark as LogIn
    Tap this the moment the driver and truck pull up at your dock. Login tells the system the truck is here and ready.
  2. 2
    Start Loading
    Now tap Start Loading. This tells the system that you — the operator — are here and ready to begin loading.
  3. 3
    Select BOL Items
    Last, tap Select BOL Items to open the list of items on the bill of lading. (Note: in the menu this sits above Start Loading, but you tap it third.)
STEP 8

Open the BOL Items

Each line on the bill of lading appears here. Counter shows what's still to load.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Header detail
    Pinned summary of the load — Load, BOL, Driver, Stage Location, Withdrawal Comment. Stays visible while you work.
  2. 2
    Remaining to Load
    Counter shows how many BOL items still need loading. Starts at the full total (7 / 7 here) and ticks down with each Load Item tap.
  3. 3
    Item rows
    Each row is one line on the bill of lading. Brown labels are field names; values appear next to them in black bold — Description, Mill, Pounds Gross, etc.
  4. 4
    Detail (i) icon
    Opens an Item Details page for that specific line — see full details, take photos, log damage, or enter a Heat Number. Covered next.
  5. 5
    Load Item
    Marks whichever rows are selected as loaded. You can pick one row at a time or several at once before tapping.
STEP 9

Select Items to Load

Tap one or more rows — selected items turn orange. Counter doesn't change until you tap Load Item.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Selected rows turn orange
    Tap any row to mark it for loading — the whole row turns orange to confirm. Tap again to deselect. You can pick several at once.
  2. 2
    Unselected rows stay white
    Rows you haven't tapped stay in their normal color. They're still waiting for you to come back to them.
  3. 3
    Counter doesn't change yet
    Selecting doesn't decrement Remaining. It still reads 7 / 7 — nothing is logged as loaded until you tap Load Item.
  4. 4
    Tap Load Item
    When the selection matches what's actually on the truck, tap Load Item. Every selected row is logged as loaded in one shot.
STEP 10

Mark Items as Loaded

After Load Item, selected rows get a green check ✓ and the counter ticks down.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Green check ✓
    Each row that was selected when you tapped Load Item now has a green check on the left. The check means that item is on the truck.
  2. 2
    Counter ticks down
    Remaining drops by the number of items just loaded. Here it went from 7 / 7 to 3 / 7 — four items loaded, three still pending.
  3. 3
    Pending rows
    Anything without a check is still waiting. Tap those rows next.
  4. 4
    Repeat until 0
    Select the next batch, tap Load Item again. The load is fully loaded when Remaining hits 0 / 7.
STEP 11

Item Details

Tap (i) on any BOL item to open this screen — photos, damage notes, and Heat Number for that specific line.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Camera (top-right)
    Opens the photo screen for this specific line item — covered next. Photos taken from here are tied to this item, not the whole load.
  2. 2
    Item details
    Read-only summary — Description, Mill, Liner Ft, Pounds Gross, Warehouse Processed, Loaded state, and how many photos are already on file.
  3. 3
    Damage Comments
    Type a short note if this item is damaged. The office sees this on the load record.
  4. 4
    Heat Number
    If the mill heat number isn't already populated, enter it here. Saves a trip back to ask the lead.
  5. 5
    Load/Unload Item
    Loads just this one item or if you are viewing an item already marked loaded, you can Unload, remove the green check, from this item.
STEP 12

Document an Item

Tapping the camera on Item Details opens this screen. Take photos of damage or scan related docs — attached to this specific line item.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Photo Type
    Pick the category before you snap or scan — POD (proof of delivery), Other Doc, Damage, Loads, or Other Photo. The default is POD.
  2. 2
    Comment
    Optional note describing what's in the photo. Useful for damage shots so the office understands what they're looking at.
  3. 3
    Scan Docs
    Use this for paperwork — bills of lading, packing slips, signed documents. The app processes them as documents (multi-page friendly).
  4. 4
    Take Photos
    Use this for photos of damage, the loaded truck, the dock, or anything else visual. Photos taken here attach to this specific line item.
STEP 13

All Items Loaded

Counter hits 0 / 7 — every row checked. Tap back to return to Load Details.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    0 / 7 Remaining
    Counter reads 0 — every line on the bill of lading has been logged as loaded. The truck has every item.
  2. 2
    Every row has ✓
    Each row shows a green check on the left. If any row were missing one, you still had items to load.
  3. 3
    Tap back
    Back arrow returns you to Load Details. From there, open the menu one more time to wrap up — POD photo, End Loading, Loading Complete.
STEP 14

Close Out: Capture the POD

Open the menu again. Tap Photo/POD to grab the signed proof of delivery.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Tap Photo/POD
    Opens the same photo screen you saw at Step 12 — but photos taken from here attach to the LOAD as a whole, not a single line item. The next slide walks through capturing the signed proof of delivery.
STEP 15

Document the Proof of Delivery (POD)

Tapping Photo/POD from the menu opens this same screen — but photos here attach to the WHOLE LOAD, not a single line item.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Photo Type
    Leave it on POD — that's the default and the right category for the signed proof of delivery. Switch to Loads if you're also documenting the loaded truck.
  2. 2
    Comment
    Optional note — e.g., “signed at gate by driver” or “customer rep refused to sign.” Helps the office interpret what they see later.
  3. 3
    Scan Docs
    Best for the signed BOL or any printed paperwork the driver hands over. The scanner crops and de-skews each page automatically.
  4. 4
    Take Photos
    Best for the signed POD itself (when there's no separate paperwork), the loaded truck, or any condition you want recorded. Photos here attach to the load, not a line item.
STEP 16

Scan the POD Document

Frame each page, the camera captures it. Multi-page POD docs supported — when done, tap the blue check to save.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Close (X)
    Cancels the whole scanner session — no pages saved. Tap if you opened it by mistake.
  2. 2
    Blue check — Done
    Saves every captured page as one document and returns you to the Photo screen. Tap this once you're satisfied with all the pages.
  3. 3
    Shutter — one tap per page
    Manual capture. Stay in the scanner, line up the next page, tap again. Each tap adds another page to the same document.
  4. 4
    Auto / Manual
    Auto captures the moment the page is framed. Manual waits for you to tap the shutter.
  5. 5
    Page thumbnails
    Every page you've captured shows up at the bottom as a small thumbnail — quick visual count of how many you've scanned so far.
STEP 17

Review Captured Pages

Quick check before saving — adjust corners, rotate, delete a bad page, or retake the whole scan.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Back
    When you're satisfied, tap back to save the scanned pages to the load record and return to the Document screen.
  2. 2
    Page indicator (e.g., “1 of 2”)
    Shows which page you're reviewing out of the total captured. Swipe left/right to flip through each page.
  3. 3
    Retake — drops every page
    Throws away ALL pages from this scanning session and reopens the camera. Use only if the whole batch is unusable.
  4. 4
    Adjust · Filters · Rotate
    Per-page tools. Adjust pulls the corner handles to fix a misaligned crop. Filters toggles color / grayscale / B&W. Rotate spins the page in 90° steps.
  5. 5
    Delete — drops only this page
    Removes just the page you're viewing. The rest of the scan stays. Handy if one page is blurry but the others are fine.
STEP 18

Everything Attached to the Load

After scanning and snapping all your photos, the Photo screen lists every document and image attached to this load.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Photo Type tabs
    Filter the cards by category — POD, Other Doc, Damage, Loads, Other Photo. The current tab is Damage in this example. Switching tabs shows just that type's items.
  2. 2
    Scan Docs / Take Photos
    Add more to the load at any time. Scan Docs for the document scanner, Take Photos for camera shots. Once added, they appear as new cards.
  3. 3
    Damage / Loads photos
    Camera shots show as thumbnails. Tap a card to view full-size, edit the comment, or delete.
  4. 4
    PDF scans (POD, Other Doc)
    Multi-page scans saved through the document scanner show with a PDF icon. Tap to view all the pages.
  5. 5
    Card metadata
    Every card shows the photo type, the system ID, the timestamp, and who captured it. The office can trace any item back to the person and moment.
STEP 19

Close Out: End Loading & Loading Complete

Stop your activity clock with End Loading, then finalize the load — and the driver's dwell timer — with Loading Complete.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Tap End Loading first
    Stops the clock on YOUR time on this load. The operator clock for this load is now closed. Loading is finished from your side; you can step away.
  2. 2
    Then tap Loading Complete
    Finalizes the load in the system and stops the driver's dwell-on-site timer. The load drops off your active list, Driver Signature becomes available on Load Details, and the office knows the truck can leave the yard.
ANYTIME

Helping on a Load

Multiple staff can share a load — and the app tracks everyone's time independently.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Anyone can join
    Each operator opens the load → Load Details → menu → Start Loading. No coordination needed; multiple staff can be on the same load at once.
  2. 2
    Everyone works in parallel
    All clocks run independently. The bars on the left show three operators with overlapping shifts on the same load.
  3. 3
    End Loading clocks you out
    Helpers tap End Loading to stop their personal clock. They can leave any time, even after just a few minutes — their hours are still logged.
  4. 4
    Lead closes with Loading Complete
    The lead operator stays past End Loading and taps Loading Complete from the action menu. That's the only action that finalizes the load in the system.

Receiving App User Guide

How to unload a rail car using the Receiving mobile app version 10.1 as of May 15, 2026 — A step-by-step walkthrough · SCM

STEP 1

Select Your Terminal

Tap the row that matches where you're working today.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Your login
    Your name and email show at the top so you know you're signed in to the right account.
  2. 2
    Screen title: Terminals
    Confirms you're on the terminal-picker screen.
  3. 3
    Power button (log out)
    Only tap this at the end of your shift. It signs you out of the app.
  4. 4
    Terminal list
    Every location your account has access to. Format is Terminal: City.
  5. 5
    Tap your terminal
    Tap the row for where you're working today. The app moves to the next screen automatically — no Next or Confirm button.
STEP 2

Find Your Equipment

Search for the rail car, container, or truck you're about to unload.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Back arrow
    Returns you to the Terminals screen if you picked the wrong one.
  2. 2
    Screen title: Receivers
    Lists every rail car, container, and inbound truck assigned to your terminal.
  3. 3
    Search field
    Tap the field and type the rail car number, container ID, or any part of the description.
  4. 4
    Clear button (×)
    Tap to wipe the search and start over.
  5. 5
    Terminal heading
    Confirms results are filtered to your terminal — here, Napa: Fontana.
  6. 6
    Match — tap to open
    The row shows carrier, equipment #, beams/qty, invoice, PO, D.Lock, and BOL#. The grey "3" badge shows the item count. Tap the row to open it.
STEP 3

Select Task

Pick the role you're doing, then tap START TASK.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Back arrow
    Returns you to the Receivers list if you opened the wrong record.
  2. 2
    Screen title: Select Task
    Confirms you've opened a receiver and now need to choose your role.
  3. 3
    Pick your task
    Multiple operators can run different tasks on the same equipment at the same time. Tap the role you're doing: Identify Product — verify what's being received. Store Product — put product away in its location. Unload — physically unload the rail car, container, or truck.
  4. 4
    Tap START TASK (top right)
    Locks in your selection and starts the clock on the task. The same button becomes END TASK when you're done.
REFERENCE

Working as a Team

Multiple operators can share an unload — and the app tracks everyone's time.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Anyone can join — Steps 1, 2, 3
    Each operator picks the same terminal, opens the same equipment, chooses a task (Identify Product, Store Product, or Unload), and taps Start Task. No coordination needed.
  2. 2
    Everyone works in parallel
    Multiple operators can be on the same equipment at the same time. They can take the same role or split up — whatever the job needs.
  3. 3
    When you're done helping — END TASK
    Support operators tap END TASK to stop their personal clock. They can leave any time, even after just a few minutes. Their hours are logged.
  4. 4
    One person closes the receiver
    The lead operator stays past END TASK to review Located items and tap Accept Receiver. That's the only step that finalizes the receiver in the system.
STEP 4

Review the Item List

Every item on the pre-receiver is shown — locate each one as you unload.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    END TASK (red, top left)
    Tap when you've finished your role on this equipment. Don't tap until you're done.
  2. 2
    Camera & File counter
    Tap the camera to take a photo (damage, seals, the load). The (File: 0) counter shows how many photos you've attached.
  3. 3
    Extras
    Add an item that isn't on the pre-receiver (something showed up that wasn't expected).
  4. 4
    Locate & Mark Missing
    Locate marks an item as found and put away. Mark Missing flags one that's not on the equipment. Both are walked through on the next slides.
  5. 5
    Progress dashboard
    Shows your terminal, the equipment, the task, and a live count: Total / Yet to locate / Located / Missing.
  6. 6
    Item rows
    Each row is an expected item — number, pieces, linear feet, weight, PO. Use Edit Weight to update weight, or the (i) icon for more detail.
STEP 5

Locate Unloaded Items

Tap the items you've just unloaded, then tap Locate.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Tap each item you've unloaded
  2. The row turns green to show it's selected. The counter at the right updates — here, 2 of 3 rows are selected.
  3. 2
    Tap Locate
  4. Once your items are highlighted, tap the Locate button at the top. The app moves you to the next screen to record where they were put.
  5. Tip: You can select multiple items and Locate them in one go.
STEP 6

Select Location

Tell the app where you put the items, then confirm.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Pick the stock location
  2. Tap the row for where you put the items. The selected row turns green so you can see your choice — here, G1.
  3. 2
    Tap Locate Items
  4. The green Locate Items button at the bottom confirms your choice. The selected items are now marked as Located at G1 and the app returns to the item list.
  5. Tip: Use the search field at the top if your list of locations is long.
STEP 7

Items Remaining

The list now shows only what's left — repeat Locate until everything is located.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Counters updated
    Located moves up by 2, Yet to locate drops to 1. The dashboard always shows where you are.
  2. 2
    Only what's left is shown
    Located items disappear from the list. Just the items you haven't found yet remain.
  3. Keep going
  4. Repeat the same two-step cycle for the remaining item(s): • Tap the row(s) you've just unloaded → tap Locate • Pick the stock location → tap Locate Items When everything is Located (or Missing), tap END TASK to finish.
REFERENCE

Inventory Details

Optional — tap the (i) icon on any item to log condition, weight, photos, and notes.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. How to open: Tap the (i) icon on any item row. Use this screen anytime to log condition, take photos, edit weight, or add comments.
  2. What you can do here
  3. 1
    Mark Condition
    Tap Good, Damaged, or Missing. Use Damaged when product arrives with visible damage so it's flagged in records.
  4. 2
    Edit Weight & take photos
    Edit Weight opens a keypad to enter the actual weight. The camera lets you attach photos of damage, seals, labels, or anything worth recording.
  5. 3
    Inventory fields
    Read-only details about the item — PO, size, length, pieces, linear feet, location, and image count. Green values are what's currently on file.
  6. 4
    Add Comment
    Leave a note — useful for damage descriptions or anything the next person should see.
REFERENCE

Documenting Damaged Items

Marking an item Damaged prompts you to attach a photo.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 83%
  2. 1
    Extra
    Damage Product Packing Tap Damaged On Inventory Details, tap the Damaged option in the Condition selector.
  3. 2
    Save
    Tap YES on the prompt The app suggests attaching a photo. Tap YES to open the photo tool.
  4. 3
    Take a Photo & describe
    Photo Type is preset to Damage. Add a Comment, then tap Take a Photo.
  5. 4
    Tap Save
    Saves the photo and comment to this item's record.
REFERENCE

Updating Item Weight

Use when a product arrives at a different weight than the pre-receiver shows.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Tap Edit Weight
    Open the item's record, then tap Edit Weight in the top right. You can also reach it from the Edit Weight button on each row of the item list.
  2. 2
    Enter the actual weight
    Type the real weight from the scale into the field. The keypad pops up automatically.
  3. 3
    Tap Save
    Updates the weight on this item. Tap Cancel to leave the weight unchanged.
  4. When to use: Any time the actual weight differs from the pre-receiver — usually after weighing the product at the scale.
STEP 8

All Items Processed — Receiver Not Yet Done

Your task list is empty, but the receiver still needs to be closed out.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Final tally — items processed
    Yet to locate is 0, but the receiver itself isn't closed yet. Two things still need to happen.
  2. 2
    SUPPORT STAFF
    Tap END TASK to stop your clock
  3. If you helped on the unload but aren't responsible for closing out the receiver, tap END TASK. It ends your time on this equipment only — the receiver itself stays open.
  4. 3
    LEAD OPERATOR (closes the receiver)
    Review Missing items (not-located icon)
  5. Tap the not-located icon (locator arrow with a slash) to see only items marked Missing. Double-check before continuing.
  6. 4
    Open Located items (➤) to close out
    Tap the paper-plane icon to open the Located list — this is where the lead operator finalizes the receiver.
STEP 9

Review Missing Items

Recover anything marked Missing by mistake.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Select any items found after all
  2. Scan the Missing list. If any of these items were actually on the equipment and were marked Missing by mistake, tap the row — it turns green to confirm.
  3. 2
    Tap Remove From Missing List
  4. Returns the selected items to the regular receiver detail list as Yet-to-Locate, so you can locate them properly. Items left here stay marked Missing.
  5. Tip: Use the (i) icon on a row first to verify item details before recovering it from the Missing list.
STEP 10

Close the Receiver

Review every located item and its stock location, then accept.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 1
    Review each item's location
    The last value on each row is the stock location. Make sure each item is where it should be — here, two items at G1 and one at G2.
  2. 2
    Drill in if you need to (i)
    Tap the info icon on any row to confirm details, photos, weight, or condition before accepting.
  3. 3
    Tap Accept Receiver
    Starts the close-out. The app will then ask two final questions to finish (see Step 11).
  4. Next: Two final confirmations After Accept Receiver, the app asks if items are Ready to Ship and whether the rail car is complete.
  5. Heads-up: Only the lead operator should tap Accept Receiver. Support staff should have tapped END TASK earlier (Step 8).
STEP 11

Two Final Confirmations

Confirm shipping status, then close out the rail car in the system.

What you'll see on this screen

  1. 88%
    FIRST DIALOG Accept Receiver
  2. Ready to Ship?
    Ready to Ship — product enters inventory immediately and is ready for outbound. Send to CSR for review — flag this receiver for a customer service rep when something looks off.
  3. SECOND DIALOG
    Is Rail car complete?
  4. Rail Car is complete — marks the rail car as unloaded in the rail system. Use when the car is empty and clear. Rail Car is NOT complete — leaves the car open in the rail system (e.g., it still needs cleaning).
  5. ✓ All done — receiver is complete.
