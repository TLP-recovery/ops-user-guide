Where the App Gets Its Lists

You check a truck in, the first thing the app asks for is a Client — and the list is empty. Nothing is broken. Every list in the app is filled in somewhere else, in Transload Pro. This page says where, and exactly what to ask for.

Why a list is empty — read this first

  • The app does not let you create clients, destinations or products in the field. That is on purpose, not a fault.
  • Those things carry the shipping terms and the inventory setup behind a load. If the field could invent them, a load could be built against terms nobody agreed.
  • So an empty list means the setup has not been done yet. It takes Customer Service a couple of minutes.
  • Every picker in the app now has a ? beside its label. Tap it and you get the same answer as this page, plus the sentence to send Customer Service.
  • Once they save it, close and reopen the picker and it will be there.
List 1

Client

The first thing the app asks for when you check a driver in.

What fills it: a BOL Header Template in Transload Pro that is marked Extended Use, with its Terminal set to the terminal you are standing at. No template, no client.

The trap: a template that exists but points at a different terminal is completely invisible here. It will look to Customer Service as though the client is set up.

Ask for: “Please add an Extended-Use BOL Header Template for [client name] at [terminal] so it appears in the bulk ops tablet.”

List 2

Destination

Where the load is going. Asked on the Header step.

What fills it: the same Extended-Use BOL Header Template as the client. Each template is one client shipping to one destination.

The trap — this one catches everybody: a second destination for the same client needs a second template. It is not a field somebody adds to the existing one. Say that plainly when you ask, or you will get told it is already set up.

Ask for: “Please add another Extended-Use BOL Header Template for [client] at [terminal] shipping to [destination].”

List 3

Product Type

What the truck is here for. Asked at check-in, after the client.

What fills it: the client's Product Profiles in Transload Pro — but only profiles set up to hold bulk stock. The profile has to be marked as volume stock or weight stock.

The trap: a profile with neither ticked exists, looks perfectly normal in Transload Pro, and is invisible to this picker.

This is not a list of what is on site. It is what the client is set up to handle, so it works before a single car has arrived. If the product is listed but no cars are, that is normal — cars show up on the Source list at the railcars step.

Ask for: “Please add a bulk Product Profile for [client] for [product], with volume stock or weight stock ticked.”

List 4

Driver

Who is driving. Asked at check-in.

What fills it: your company's staff records in Transload Pro whose Employee Type is a Driver type. Drivers marked on leave, and deleted records, are hidden on purpose.

It is your whole company, not just this terminal — the list can be long. Type part of the name into the search box rather than scrolling.

Ask for an email address to go on the record. It is worth having even if it has to be a work address rather than the driver's own.

Ask for: “Please add [driver name] to the driver list with an Employee Type of Driver, phone [number] and email [address].”

List 5

Source — railcars, tanks, bulk storage

What you are drawing from. Asked on the railcars step.

This one is different from the other four. It is live inventory, not setup — the cars, tanks and ground storage actually holding product at your terminal right now.

A car is missing: a car appears once its receiver has been posted in Transload Pro and it still has volume left. A car sitting on the ground but not received yet will not be here. That is the usual reason.

A car that empties and drops off the list is correct behaviour, not a fault.

Ask for: “Please check the receiver for car [car number] has been posted at [terminal] — it is not showing as a source in the bulk ops tablet.”
